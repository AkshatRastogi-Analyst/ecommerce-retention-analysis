Which channels bring the best customers?

Who brings the money?

Customers ranked from biggest spender to smallest. The dashed line shows what an equal split would look like.

One-time vs repeat customers

Most customers buy once, but the ones who return bring a much larger share of the money.

How long until the next order?

Days between one delivered order and the next, for customers who came back.

Customer segments (RFM)

Cohort retention

Each row is a group of customers who made their first purchase in that month. Month 1 shows how many ordered again the next month. Brighter gold = more customers came back (Month 0 is always 100%, so later months use their own colour scale).
